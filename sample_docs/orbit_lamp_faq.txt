Orbit Smart Lamp - Frequently Asked Questions (fictional sample product)

How do I pair the Orbit lamp with my phone?
Install the Orbit Home app, switch the lamp on, and hold the touch ring for 5 seconds until the light pulses blue. Then tap "Add device" in the app. Pairing uses Bluetooth first and then moves the lamp to your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network. 5 GHz networks are not supported.

What is the battery life?
The Orbit lamp has a 4,000 mAh battery. At 50% brightness it lasts about 18 hours; at full brightness it lasts about 7 hours. A full charge over USB-C takes roughly 3 hours with a 10 W adapter.

How do I reset the lamp?
Hold the touch ring for 15 seconds until the light flashes red three times. This removes the Wi-Fi settings and unlinks the lamp from your account.

What is the warranty?
The Orbit lamp has a 2-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. The battery is covered for 1 year. Water damage and physical damage are not covered.

Can I return the lamp?
Unused lamps in original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Opened lamps can be returned within 14 days, minus a 10% restocking fee.

Does the lamp work with voice assistants?
Yes. The lamp supports Google Assistant and Amazon Alexa through the Orbit Home skill. Apple HomeKit is not supported in the current firmware (version 2.3).
