# Northstar Labs Employee Handbook (fictional sample)

This handbook belongs to Northstar Labs, a fictional company used to demonstrate document Q&A.

## Working hours

Core working hours are 11:00 to 16:00 IST, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours, employees may
plan their own schedule as long as they work 40 hours per week. Meetings should not be scheduled
before 10:00 or after 18:00 without the attendees' agreement.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Fully remote arrangements need written
approval from the department head and are reviewed every six months. The company reimburses
internet costs of up to ₹1,000 per month for remote days.

## Leave policy

Every full-time employee receives 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 8 unused
days can be carried over to the next year; carried-over days expire on 31 March. Sick leave is
separate: employees get 12 days of paid sick leave per year, and a medical certificate is
required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

Parental leave is 26 weeks for the primary caregiver and 4 weeks for the secondary caregiver.

## Learning budget

Each employee has an annual learning budget of ₹40,000 for courses, books and conferences.
Requests above ₹15,000 for a single item need manager approval before purchase. Unused budget
does not carry over.

## Equipment

New joiners receive a laptop on their first day. Laptops are refreshed every three years. Lost or
damaged equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours.
